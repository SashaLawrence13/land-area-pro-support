# Land Area Pro privacy policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

Land Area Pro does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal information. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

Location. The app asks for your location so it can show where you are on the map and, in Walk mode, record a boundary as you walk it. Your location is used on your phone only. It is never sent to the developer or to anyone else. Background location is used only while you are recording a walk, so the recording keeps going when the screen is off. You can turn location off in iPhone Settings at any time. The map still works and you can still drop points by hand.

Saved plots. The names, notes, photos, colors and coordinates you save stay on your device. If you back up your iPhone, they may be part of that backup under your own Apple account settings. Deleting a plot, or deleting the app, removes it from your phone.

Photos. If you attach a photo to a plot, the app asks for access to choose a photo. The photo is copied into the app on your device and is not uploaded.

Maps. The map pictures come from Apple Maps. Apple's own privacy policy covers how Apple handles map requests.

Exports. When you export a PDF, KML, GeoJSON or CSV file, the file goes where you choose to send it, such as Mail, Files or another app. Land Area Pro does not see or keep it.

Children. The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new date.
