# Land Area Pro support

Questions, bug reports and ideas are welcome at sashalawrence100@yahoo.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were measuring if it helps.

## Common questions

How do I measure a field? Open the app, stay on Area, and tap the satellite map to drop a corner at each bend of the boundary. The area and perimeter update as you add corners. Touch and hold a corner, then drag, to move it. Tap the small circle in the middle of an edge to add a corner there.

How do I change the unit? Tap the unit next to the big number, or tap one of the quick unit buttons under it. Tap More to see every unit, including regional ones.

My local unit has a different size than the one in the app. Many land units change by state or district. Open the unit, pick the variant used where the land is, and tap the info button to see the size and the source. If yours is not listed, choose Add a custom unit and enter its size in square meters, acres or any unit in the list.

How does Walk mode work? Choose Walk, wait for the GPS accuracy dot to turn green, tap Start walking, and walk the edge of the land. Tap Finish when you are back at the start. The shape closes and you can still drag corners to clean it up. Walk mode keeps recording with the screen off. Open areas with a clear sky give the best accuracy.

How accurate is it? The math is geodesic and checked against known shapes. The result is only as good as where the corners are. On the satellite map that depends on the imagery. With GPS it depends on the signal, which is usually a few feet in the open. For legal boundaries, use a licensed surveyor.

How do I share a measurement? Save the plot, swipe left on it in the Saved plots list, tap Details, then choose PDF, KML, GeoJSON or CSV.

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.
